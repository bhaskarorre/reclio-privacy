Reclio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Reclio is a screen recorder and video editor that runs entirely on your phone. Nothing you record, edit or set up in Reclio leaves your phone.

What Reclio does not do

  • It does not create an account, and does not ask for your name or email to work.
  • It does not upload, stream or back up your recordings, screenshots or audio. Everything you capture is saved on this phone, in Movies/Reclio, Pictures/Reclio and Music/Reclio, until you move or delete it.
  • It contains no advertising, no analytics, and no usage or telemetry tracking. Nothing is sent when you record, edit or export.
  • It does not read your other photos, videos or files. When you add an image or a music track to an edit, Android's own picker hands Reclio that one file — which is why Reclio asks for no storage permission at all.

What is stored on your phone

  • Recordings, screenshots and exports — in Movies/Reclio, Pictures/Reclio and Music/Reclio. These are visible to your gallery and to other apps, the same as anything else you save there.
  • Settings — in Reclio's private app storage.
  • Editor projects — the cuts, crops, zooms and captions you have made for a recording, in Reclio's private app storage. They describe what to do with a recording; they never contain video or audio. Deleting a recording in Reclio deletes its project.
  • A diagnostic log — in Reclio's private app storage, capped in size and overwritten over time. It never leaves the phone.

Only your settings are included in Android's cloud backup. Recordings, projects and logs are not.

Permissions, and what each is for

PermissionWhy
Screen capture (granted per recording, through Android's own dialog) To record the screen. Android asks every time; Reclio cannot record without it and cannot keep the grant for later.
MicrophoneOnly if you turn the microphone on. Audio is written into your recording and nowhere else.
CameraOnly for the camera bubble and camera-only mode.
Display over other apps The countdown, the camera preview, the timer, pause and stop buttons and drawing tools, and bringing Reclio back to the front when a recording ends. Optional — the notification does the same job.
NotificationsThe ongoing recording notification, which Android requires, and its pause and stop actions.
Alarms and remindersOnly if you set a scheduled recording.
Run at startupSo a scheduled recording survives a restart.
Keep awakeSo a long recording is not cut short by the phone sleeping.
InternetOnly to load the page you type into Website mode.
Google Play billingTo ask Google Play whether you have Reclio Pro, and to open Play's purchase screen.

Reclio does not use an accessibility service and does not request access to manage all files. Two settings change something outside the app while you record: "Silence the speaker" mutes the media stream and unmutes it when the recording ends, and "Play the recording afterwards" opens the finished file in whichever player you choose. Both are off unless you turn them on.

Paying for Reclio Pro

Reclio Pro is sold through Google Play. Google Play runs the 14-day free trial, takes the payment, and handles renewals, cancelling and refunds, under Google's own privacy policy. Reclio never receives your card, UPI or bank details. It only asks Google Play whether this Google account has Reclio Pro, and does not keep the answer.

Children

Reclio is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
